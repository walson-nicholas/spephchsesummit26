About the summit

A two-day forum on HSE excellence in Nigeria’s energy growth

The HSE Summit 2026 brings together professionals, industry practitioners, regulators, academics, students and other stakeholders to examine how Nigeria’s energy industry can grow production while maintaining strong health, safety and environmental standards.

The programme focuses on knowledge sharing, technical engagement and practical learning, with summit-only, hybrid training and practical training registration options.

Nigeria can produce more — but never at the cost of lives, safety or the environment.

Key Focus Areas

  • HSE Leadership
  • Process & Occupational Safety
  • Contractor Management
  • Asset Integrity
  • Emergency Preparedness
  • Environmental Stewardship
  • Workforce Competence
  • Technology & Innovation
  • Regulatory Compliance
  • Lessons from Incidents
